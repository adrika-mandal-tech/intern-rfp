# Your work goes here

Everything you produce lives in this folder. Nothing outside it should need editing.

Your two annotation worklists are already here, pre-drawn and waiting:

- `worklist_A_random.jsonl` — 40 rows, a fair sample across the RFP
- `worklist_B_lockbox.jsonl` — 20 rows, Lockbox questionnaire only

Keep them as separate files and never combine their numbers.
[Task 1 §4](../03-tasks/task-1-expand-golden-set.md) explains why.

## Day one

Copy two templates in and start them:

```
cp ../04-templates/research-log-template.md research-log.md
cp ../04-templates/questions-template.md questions.md
```

Then add to both daily. Five minutes a day, and together they're what makes your work
reviewable by someone who wasn't watching you do it.

## Found something wrong outside this folder?

Don't fix it — **write it down** in `questions.md` under *Disagreements*. An error you
found is a finding worth reporting; an error you silently corrected is invisible to
everyone. [Task 5](../03-tasks/task-5-explainer-review.md) collects these properly at
the end.
