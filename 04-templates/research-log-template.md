# Research log

Copy this to `your-work/research-log.md` on day one and add to it daily.

Three lines a day is fine. The point isn't detail — it's that someone who wasn't
watching can reconstruct what happened and why. Write it at the end of each day, not
retrospectively on Friday.

**Log the dead ends too.** "Spent the morning on X, it went nowhere because Y" is one of
the more useful lines you can write, and it's the one people skip.
