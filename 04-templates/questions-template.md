# Questions

Copy to `your-work/questions.md` and keep it running from day one.

**Write things down the moment they're unclear**, even if you resolve them yourself an
hour later. The ones you resolve yourself are often the most useful entries — they mark
exactly where this pack failed to explain something, and that's a defect worth fixing
for whoever comes next.

Bring this list to check-ins.

## Disagreements

Things in this pack you think are wrong, unclear, or badly reasoned. Separate from
questions, because they need a different response.

Don't soften these. "Task 1 §6 Rule 1 contradicts the example in §5" is exactly the kind
of thing we need to hear, and you're the only person positioned to notice it.
