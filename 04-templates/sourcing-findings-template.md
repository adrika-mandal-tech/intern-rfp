# Sourcing findings — Task 2

Copy to `your-work/sourcing-findings.md`. This is the write-up that sits on top of the
log; the log is the raw record, this is what someone reads.

---

## 1. Outcome

One paragraph. What did you find, at what tier, and how confident are you in it?

If the answer is "no pairs, but here's a map of where they aren't", say that plainly in
the first sentence. That's a legitimate outcome (Task 2 §3) and burying it helps nobody.

## 2. What I found

Per document, if any:

- What it is, who published it, where
- Why you're confident it's a genuine submitted proposal (Task 2 §7 check 2)
- Whether the matching RFP exists, and where
- Page count and rough content — is there enough in it to retrieve from?

## 3. Which sources publish received proposals — and which don't

**The most reusable part of this task.** Someone should be able to read this section and
know where to go without repeating your work.

| Source | Publishes RFPs | Publishes received proposals | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

Then a short paragraph: **is there a pattern?** Does it track state, agency type,
procurement size, sector? Do smaller agencies publish more freely than big ones? A
pattern here is worth far more than any single document, because it tells the next
person where to look rather than what was found.

## 5. Obstacles

Anything that blocked you — logins, paywalls, rate limiting, sites that refused
downloads, documents that turned out not to be documents. Note which ones are *hard*
blocks and which might yield to a different approach.

## 6. What I could not determine

Be specific. "I couldn't establish whether <state> publishes proposals, because their
portal requires a search by solicitation number and I couldn't enumerate them" is a
useful handoff. "Didn't get to it" is fine too, if that's what happened.

## 7. If I had another week

Where would you look next, and why? Rank them. What made you pick that order?

This is the section that gets read most closely if someone picks the work up after you —
you'll have built up intuition about where these documents live that isn't written
anywhere else.
